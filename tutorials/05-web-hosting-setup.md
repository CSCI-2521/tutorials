# Setting up web hosting services


# Part 1: Create an online database

## Sign up at `neon.com`

Neon provides free permanent databases.

1. Visit https://neon.com and click "Get Started"
2. Sign up with Google or a username and password (Google is recommended). Again I recommend using your personal account (not university) so you can automatically keep it after graduating.
3. Neon will take you through a setup process. Name your "Organization" anything you want, e.g.
<img width="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d2571262-a2a7-402d-b16b-a73bc3572903" />

4. Click "Next" in the bottom right.
5. On the next page, name your database something like "Demo database 1":
<img width="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/26470c72-6fac-419a-9fc6-0a52c379133f" />

6. Click "Create project" in the bottom right.
7. You will see an agent prompt. Copy this and email it to yourself as "Neon setup prompt" for later:
<img width="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6271933c-0ec2-4ff9-8a95-c78e4487dc5f" />

8. Click Next/Continue/Create in the bottom right.

---

*You now have an online database. You're ready to get an app server.*

---

# Part 2: Sign up for an app server. 

We will use render for hosting your app server.
1. Register for an account at Render.com. Google is recommended. Personal account is recommended.
2. When you are logged in, you will likely see the Dashboard offering to set up a service. Select "Web service"

<img width="950" height="361" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2a950f40-b8e5-45c0-8044-1fd8f651d289" />

3. If you don't see this, click the top left Render icon on the page and 
3. Click "GitHub" to connect your github account to Render, then click "Authorize" in the next window.
4. The next window will ask you where to "install" render (on github.com). Click your personal account name.
<img height="300" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e107204-5557-4df7-a55e-cf56971edaf3" />

5. You are done for now. We will create a web service later.
