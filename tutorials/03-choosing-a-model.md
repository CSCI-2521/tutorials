# How to choose which AI model to use
## Model size vs intelligence

Smarter models *tend* to be larger.

Larger models *tend* to cost more.

Proprietary models *tend* to be smarter.

Proprietary models are much more expensive *if you pay per token*.

Proprietary models are much *less* expensive *if you use a subscription*.

## Leaderboard links

These are just a few Benchmark/Leaderboard sites for comparing model performance against price, speed, proprietary vs. open-weights.

- [Arena.ai](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent). People (not benchmarks) score agents head-to-head on different tasks.
- [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/). Leaderboard comparing benchmark performance in different areas.
- [LiveBench](https://livebench.ai). Another leaderboard with slightly different benchmarks.
- [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/). Another leaderboard; fewer benchmarks, gives a single "intelligence index" and a "Cost per task".
